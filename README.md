## What I do

I can talk through a P&L and a system design in the same meeting. I turn business goals into technical decisions, and I explain technical constraints in terms leadership can act on.

Lately a lot of that work is AI. Most companies have a few places where a model is worth what it costs. I find those and build them, and I'll tell you when a simpler tool would do the job.

## Background

I've built and run technology functions at multiple companies over the years, often starting from nothing. That has covered infrastructure, security, and the internal systems each business depends on. I've acted as CISO, managed vendors, and automated a good share of the back office along the way.

As a founder and fractional CTO, I've taken products from first spec to production. That usually means writing the data model and product specs, setting the architecture, and then hiring and running the team that builds it. I also build and run software of my own, so I know what it takes to keep a product working after launch.

## How I work

- Code is the last step. By the time a developer starts, the specs and the data model are settled, so the build is execution instead of discovery.
- I write decisions down: what we chose, why, and what would make us change course. Six months later, nobody has to guess.
- You get my honest read, even when it's "don't build this."
